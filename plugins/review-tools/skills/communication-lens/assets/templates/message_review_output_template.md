## 5. Minimal Revision

Preserve the user's voice as much as possible.

## 6. Warmer Revision

Make the relationship cue and low-pressure framing slightly warmer.

## 7. Short Version, If Needed

Use when the channel has a tight character limit.

## 8. Follow-Up Message After They Reply

Provide only if useful.

## 9. Boundary Note

Mention any non-writing boundary, such as legal/immigration advice limits,
confidentiality, or an overly burdensome ask.
