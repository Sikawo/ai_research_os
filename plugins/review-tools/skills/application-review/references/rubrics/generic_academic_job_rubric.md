# Generic Academic Job Rubric

Use this when no institution-specific academic job rubric has been approved.

## Core Criteria

- Independent research identity.
- Fit to department, institute, or search area.
- Originality and future trajectory.
- Evidence of productivity and fundability.
- Teaching or mentoring fit when relevant.
- Collaboration potential without dependence.
- Clarity for both specialists and non-specialists.
- Package consistency across cover letter, research statement, teaching
  statement, CV, and references.

## Sprint QC Checks

- Research identity appears early.
- Future program is feasible for the appointment level.
- Institution fit is specific but not over-personalized.
- Claims are supported by authorized source material.
- Publication status is consistent across all documents.
