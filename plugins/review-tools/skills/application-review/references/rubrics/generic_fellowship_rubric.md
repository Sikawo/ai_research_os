# Generic Fellowship Rubric

Use this when no target-specific fellowship rubric has been approved yet.

## Core Criteria

- Applicant trajectory and promise.
- Research training or independence plan.
- Mentor, host, or institutional fit where relevant.
- Project significance and feasibility.
- Development plan and future career logic.
- Evidence of productivity.
- Clarity, maturity, and specificity of writing.

## QC Checks

- Eligibility and career-stage rules verified.
- Required mentor or host documents present.
- Training, research, and career statements are consistent.
- Publication status is consistent across CV and proposal.
- Formatting and submission requirements are verified.

## Sprint Architecture Checks

- The applicant's trajectory is visible before technical detail.
- Independence, training, or host fit is calibrated to fellowship type.
- Ambition is clear without implying unsupported resources or guarantees.
- Career-development claims match the authorized source documents.
