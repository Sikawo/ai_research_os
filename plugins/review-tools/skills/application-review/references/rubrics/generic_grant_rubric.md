# Generic Grant Rubric

Use this as the mandatory universal baseline for grant proposals. A
target-specific rubric may add requirements, weights, institutional priorities,
or reviewer overlays, but it must not replace this baseline.

For grant-proposal `GatedSprint` runs, load this rubric together with:

```text
plugins/review-tools/skills/application-review/references/core/proposal_completeness_significance_citation_gate.md
```

## Core Criteria

- Background and field-context clarity.
- Critical knowledge-gap clarity.
- Significance of the problem.
- Human-health, technical, or societal relevance when relevant to the target
  program.
- Originality and conceptual advance.
- Strength of preliminary evidence.
- Mechanistic clarity.
- Aim-specific rationale and significance.
- Feasibility and risk management.
- Investigator independence and fit.
- Environment, resources, and mission fit.
- Expected outputs and decision points.
- Literature anchoring for established prior knowledge, prior findings,
  field-consensus claims, and foundational concepts.
- Clarity of writing and reviewer readability.

## QC Checks

- Required sections present.
- Required proposal functions present even when section headings differ.
- Structural Completeness Verdict included in grant-proposal approval packets.
- Background, gap, significance, and aim-specific rationale are explicit enough
  that a reviewer does not need to supply unstated logic.
- Human-health, technical, or societal relevance is explicit or marked `Not
  applicable with justification`.
- Paragraph-level prior-knowledge blocks are supported by nearby references
  when they support proposal rationale, significance, interpretation, or design.
- Page limits and formatting verified from official source.
- Budget and staffing constraints verified.
- Project order supports the narrative spine.
- Publication status consistent across documents.
- Figures, tables, and references are consistent.

## Sprint Architecture Checks

- The application reads as one coherent program rather than a list of projects.
- The strongest preliminary evidence is close enough to the central claim.
- High-risk extensions are decision-gated or moved to future directions.
- Investigator independence is visible without overstating available evidence.
- The proposal-wide significance chain connects problem, known field state,
  unknown gap, consequence, proposed work, and expected change.
- Each Aim explains why the question matters, what will be learned, and how the
  result advances the overall program beyond the immediate system.
