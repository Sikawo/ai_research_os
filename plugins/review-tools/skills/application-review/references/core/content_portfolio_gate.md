# Content Portfolio Gate

## Purpose

Classify the application's major content before restructuring or polishing.
This prevents attractive but weakly supported material from crowding out the
central story.

## Classification Labels

- `Core`: central to the application identity and should be visible early.
- `Supporting`: useful evidence or context, but not the narrative spine.
- `Decision-gated extension`: potentially valuable, but only if scope and risk
  are justified.
- `Future direction`: appropriate for trajectory, outlook, or long-term vision.
- `Remove`: distracting, unsupported, redundant, or harmful to review clarity.

## Required Scores

Use a 1 to 5 score unless the evidence is missing. These are portfolio-value
scores, not global application readiness. For eligible scientific documents,
apply `plugins/review-tools/skills/application-review/references/core/scientific_argument_gate.md` separately.
A portfolio 5 means central strength, not absence of defects or perfect argument
quality. Score risk separately as 1 = low risk through 5 = high risk, with an
explicit reason; do not apply the strength labels below to risk or average
opposite-direction scales. Missing evidence receives no numeric score.

| Score | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | weak or harmful |
| 2 | below target |
| 3 | acceptable but not compelling |
| 4 | strong |
| 5 | central strength |
| missing | cannot score from authorized material |

Score each item for:

- strategic value;
- evidence strength;
- reviewer clarity;
- fit to rubric;
- feasibility;
- independence value;
- risk.

## Output

Use `plugins/review-tools/skills/application-review/assets/templates/content_portfolio_matrix_template.md`.
Save the completed matrix in the application-specific internal folder when one
exists.
