# Revision Decision Log Template

Use this log to prevent repeated-advice loops. Keep entries short and avoid
private scientific detail unless the file is explicitly approved for that
content.

## Advice Status Labels

- `new`
- `deepened`
- `already known`
- `adopted`
- `rejected`
- `retired`
- `needs evidence`

## Log

| Date | Sprint step / mode | Advice / decision | Status | Rationale | Follow-up |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| YYYY-MM-DD | `[step or mode]` | `[short advice]` | new | `[why]` | `[next step]` |
