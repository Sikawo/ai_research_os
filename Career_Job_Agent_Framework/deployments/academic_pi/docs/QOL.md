# Compensation and QOL assessment

Scientific fit, eligibility, opportunity quality, and QOL are independent
dimensions. QOL does not change Tier 1 or Tier 2 unless private preferences
explicitly define a hard blocker.

Verified-open Tier 1 and Tier 2 roles trigger assessment or refresh. Watchlist
roles retain readily available salary facts without a full assessment by
default. Missing salary, tax, housing, childcare, education, transport, or
commute evidence remains `unknown` or `pending`; no score is fabricated.

Trusted host connectors provide dated evidence and assumptions through the
private overlay. The pure QOL module preserves 9- versus 12-month basis, keeps
local currency primary, excludes Australian employer super from spendable
salary, and computes a budget surplus only when every required cost is known.

Refresh occurs for a newly actionable role, material salary/worksite change,
configured staleness, or `refresh-qol JOB`. This output is planning support,
not tax, legal, financial, education, or housing advice.
